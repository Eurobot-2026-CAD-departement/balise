this folder contains the CAD files of the remote computing device made during the
eurobot 2026 year the device contains two Raspberry Pi cameras , a raspberry Pi 5
and a laptop stand needed to start and monitor the robot during the game 

IMPORTANT !!!

if you open the CAD you will notice that the cameras are both fixed in a certain
angle .
to determine those positions we used blender to determine the needed camera angle 
to cover the whole map at the yellow and blue side .
since each camera in blender has a field of view composed of 4 points we took the
coordinates of three of those points add to that the coordinates of the middle of
the lens , using the latter we positionned the camera , we then used the other three
points of the FOV to create a plane in solidworks that should be parallel to the 
camera lens that will therfore determine the exact camera angle needed .
